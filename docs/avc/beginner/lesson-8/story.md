        In the morning, we will have breakfast at the hotel; we will eat potatoes from a small plate. There we will walk a lot. We are going to go on a hike and sightseeing tour. We will see landmarks.<br><br>
        Outside, I take in the fresh air. In the garden, there is a piece of dark wood and a red leaf. I am going to buy a nice gift for my neighbor.<br><br>
        In the evening, we will watch football or a good movie. At night, we will close the door and get good rest. We will stay there for one week.
