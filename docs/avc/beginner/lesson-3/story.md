        Today is a good day. My friend Artur is looking at his calendar.<br><br>
        His family is big, and his house is beautiful. His father is a doctor and wants to work in a hospital. His brother is a school principal. He loves his homeland.<br><br>
        Artur's profession is a teacher. He loves teaching at school. A boy and a girl study in his classroom, and they want to listen to their teacher.<br><br>
        Artur's son and daughter are in the garden. They like to play and run. Suddenly, a little hedgehog comes near their feet. Artur wants to help them.<br><br>
        He opens the curtain and sees a beautiful rose. On the kitchen table, there is bread, salt, and walnuts. He washes his hands with soap.<br><br>
        A hunter is looking for gold on the shore of a big ocean. He feels the wind. This is a good start for this beautiful day.
