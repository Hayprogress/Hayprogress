        Today is a warm summer, and the weather is very nice and sunny. The sky is clear, but sometimes it gets very hot. I put on my summer dress and other clothes.<br><br>
        We want to have lunch outside; we eat fish, tomatoes, and wheat bread.<br><br>
        Then autumn comes, and the air turns cold. An autumn day is often cloudy. The wind picks up, and the city becomes windy. We see lightning and can hear thunder. Soon it rains; I love rainy nights.<br><br>
        Then winter comes. The winter road is often snowy because a lot of snow falls. When it is very cold, my hands can ache from the cold. I take my suitcase to travel.<br><br>
        But finally spring arrives. The springtime light is very beautiful.
