1. The comparison ladder

Imagine two towers. One is tall. Another is taller. One more is the tallest of all. Armenian does the same thing in three steps, like a ladder.

Rung Armenian English
1. Plain word մեծ big
2. More ավելի մեծ bigger
3. Most ամենամեծ the biggest
  • ավելի = "more". It is its own word, placed before the adjective, with a space.
  • ամենա- = "the most". It is a prefix, a piece that sticks to the front like glue, with no space.

Worked example: մեծ (big)

  1. Start with the plain word. մեծ means "big". Nothing added. This is the first rung.
  2. Want "bigger"? ավելի means "more". Put it in front and leave a space. "More big" = bigger. → ավելի մեծ
  3. Want "the biggest"? ամենա means "the most". Glue it onto the front, no space. → ամենամեծ
  4. Check yourself. Space = two words = "bigger". No space = one stuck word = "the biggest".

Worked example: տխուր (sad)

  1. Plain word: տխուր = sad.
  2. Add ավելի and a space: ավելի տխուր = sadder.
  3. Glue on ամենա: ամենատխուր = the saddest.

Good news

English changes its words (big, bigger, biggest; good, better, best; beautiful, more beautiful). Armenian never changes the adjective itself. Even լավ (good) and վատ (bad) follow the same rule: ավելի լավ, ամենալավ, ավելի վատ, ամենավատ.

Two traps

  1. ավելի goes before the adjective, never after.
  2. ամենա is glued. ամենա մեծ with a space is wrong.

2. Which adjectives can climb the ladder?

Adjectives come in two kinds:

  • Qualitative adjectives tell how something is (big, beautiful, sad). You can have more or less of them.
  • Relative adjectives are built from other words by adding endings (think "wooden", made from wood). You can't be "more wooden".

Only qualitative adjectives have degrees of comparison.

Bonus: the -ագույն ending

Sometimes "the most" is made with the ending -ագույն instead of the prefix. Example: լավագույն = the best.

3. Synonyms: words that mean almost the same

Think of two paths to the same playground. Different words, nearly the same meaning.

# Word Synonym
1 գեղեցիկ / beautiful սիրուն / pretty
2 տաք / warm, hot շոգ / hot (weather)
3 մեծ / big խոշոր / large
4 բարձր / tall վերև / high
5 լավ / good հրաշալի / wonderful
6 ծանր / heavy դժվար / difficult
7 քաղցր / sweet, sugary անուշ / sweet
8 բարակ / thin նեղ / narrow

Worked example

Question: "another word for մեծ (big)?"

  1. Say what it means: "big".
  2. Find the pair in the list with "big" (row 3).
  3. Its partner is խոշոր (large). Done.

Note: a few pairs (ծանր / դժվար, բարակ / նեղ, բարձր / վերև) are looser matches than true twins. Learn them as the lesson gives them.

4. Antonyms: opposites

Hot and cold, big and small. Like the two ends of a seesaw.

# Word Antonym
1 գեղեցիկ / beautiful տգեղ / ugly
2 տաք / hot ցուրտ / cold
3 մեծ / big փոքր / small
4 բարձր / high ցածր / low
5 լավ / good վատ / bad
6 ծանր / heavy թեթև / light
7 քաղցր / sweet դառը / bitter
8 բարակ / thin հաստ / thick

Worked example

Question: "the opposite of ծանր?"

  1. It means "heavy".
  2. What is the other end of the seesaw from heavy? Light.
  3. Answer: թեթև.
